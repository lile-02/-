# 小星星 — 汪苏泷

Logline: A soft-hearted original lead loves someone who is already walking away, and keeps a paper star for them anyway.

World: one night city. Indigo roofs, a rain-blue street, a cream room, a teal shore, a rose-empty stage. The only hard light is the gold star.

Motif: a paper star. It falls into their hands, tries to leave, rides in a bottle, lands on a windowsill, and sits in the empty chair. The ending puts the same star back in the sky that opened the film.

Lead: an original watercolour anime figure. Ink-blue bob, one gold streak, cream coat, rose scarf, a stitched star on the chest. Not Clawd. The person who leaves is only a rose coat seen from behind, with warm brown hair and no face, so they are not a copy of anyone.

Arc: hopeful → too late → trying to let go → still worrying → deciding to love anyway → playing alone → a small star left in the chest, and one in the sky.

Song grid: 136 BPM, filmed on the half-time pulse (68 BPM, offset 0.36s). Cuts sit on the lyric lines. Karaoke carries the words. No signs.

Shots follow the lyric starts in `src/scenes/film.js`. The long gap after "我还是为你担心" (85.6–103.3) is the tide: they sit, a wave nearly takes the bottle, they nudge it back out to sea, then look up.

Rhyme: iris opens on the sky star; iris closes on the same star after they have one of their own.
